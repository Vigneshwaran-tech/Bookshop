FAQs

Who actually sends my books?

The bookshop that stocks them. An order from two shops arrives as two parcels, each packed by that shop.

Can I collect in store?

Yes. Choose “Collect from bookshop” at checkout and we'll email you when it's ready, usually within two days.

A title is sold out. Now what?

Ask the bookseller. Most shops can special-order a title in one to three weeks.

Do you gift wrap?

Brown paper, string and a handwritten card for S$3 a book.
